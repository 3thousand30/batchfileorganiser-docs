# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: April 2026**

Batch File Organiser is developed by [3thousand30](https://3thousand30.com).

---

## What we collect

3thousand30 does not collect, store, or receive your personal files, file contents, folder paths, duplicate information, or app settings through this app.

- No analytics sent to us
- No in-app account or registration required
- No cloud sync operated by us
- No file contents uploaded to our servers

---

## What may leave your device

Batch File Organiser performs file organisation, duplicate detection, preview generation, and undo handling locally on your Windows device.

The app may also request the **Inter** user-interface font from **Google Fonts** so the app looks consistent across devices. That font request is made directly from your device to Google's servers and may include standard network information such as your IP address and browser or app request metadata, subject to Google's own privacy practices.

If Google Fonts is unavailable, the app can fall back to local system fonts.

3thousand30 does not receive your files or the contents of those Google font requests.

---

## Data stored on your device

The app may store limited local data on your device to support normal operation, such as:

- Theme preference
- Undo history for the most recent operation
- Temporary undo storage for duplicate files moved aside during an apply operation

This information remains on your device unless you choose to share it.
