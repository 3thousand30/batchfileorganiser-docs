# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: April 2026**

BatchFile Organiser is developed by [3thousand30](https://3thousand30.com).

---

## What we collect

**Nothing.** 3thousand30 does not collect, store, transmit, or have access to your personal data through this app.

- No analytics
- No telemetry sent to us
- No account or registration required
- No cloud sync operated by us

---

## What leaves your machine

**Nothing.** BatchFile Organiser works locally on your Windows device.

It reads files from folders you choose, analyzes them locally, and applies changes locally on your machine. File contents, filenames, folder paths, duplicate information, and settings are not transmitted to our servers.

---

## Data stored on your device

The app may store limited local data on your device to support normal operation, such as:

- Theme preference
- Undo history for the most recent operation
- Temporary undo storage for duplicate files moved aside during an apply operation

This information remains on your device.
